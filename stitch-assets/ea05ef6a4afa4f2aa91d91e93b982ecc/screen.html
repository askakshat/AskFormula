\mathcal{L} = T - V

Principle of stationary action yielding the Euler-Lagrange equations of motion for generalized coordinates.

Verified 100% 3,420 solved today
Analytical Mechanics Proof

Derivation: Euler-Lagrange Equation

Q.E.D.
Step 1: Action integral variation $\delta S = \delta \int_{t_1}^{t_2} \mathcal{L}(q, \dot{q}, t) \, dt = 0$
Step 2: Taylor expansion and integration by parts $\int_{t_1}^{t_2} \left( \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial q} - \frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \dot{q}} \right) \delta q \, dt = 0$
Conclusion: Fundamental Lemma of Calculus of Variations $\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \dot{q}}\right) - \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial q} = 0$
