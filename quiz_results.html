Q3

Calculate the change in entropy (\(\Delta S\)) for the isothermal expansion of an ideal gas.

cancel
Your Answer

\(\Delta S = nR \ln\left(\frac{P_2}{P_1}\right)\)

Correct Answer

\(\Delta S = nR \ln\left(\frac{V_2}{V_1}\right)\)

lightbulb

Remember that for isothermal expansion, pressure is inversely proportional to volume (\(P_1V_1 = P_2V_2\)). The formula using pressure should be \(\Delta S = nR \ln(P_1/P_2)\).
